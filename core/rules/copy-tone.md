# Copy & Tone Rules (always apply)

Applies to all UI text, notifications, empty states, errors, and App Store text.

- **Gentle, never guilt:** "Welcome back — what's one thing from today?", not "You missed 3 days" or "Don't break your streak!"
- Streaks celebrate showing up — never count down, warn, or "reset to 0" punishingly.
- Short and calm: one idea per sentence, few exclamation marks, no emoji in text we write (users may type their own).
- Errors are honest and reassuring: what happened, whether data is safe, what to do next. Every error message must be clear in meaning and must not claim anything that may be untrue (for example "nothing was saved" when part of it was). If unsure, say less, not something confusing.
- Address the user as "you"; avoid jargon ("sync", "database", "entity").
- Localization-ready: no concatenated sentence fragments; leave room for longer translations.
